1.  Team Values 

   - a.  We prioritize respect, accountability, communication, and reliability in all of our collaboration. Our team is committed to inclusion and fairness, ensuring everyone contributes equally and feels valued. 

2.  Meeting times: 

   - a.  We will meet 2 times a week on Tuesdays from 12:30-2 pm in COM 109 and on Thursdays at 12: 30 - 2 PM  in COM 109. If someone is unable to attend, we will try to get them on zoom, if that is not possible, we will provide them with what we did during our meetings so they stay up to date. 

3.  Communication: 

   - a.  To communicate to spread group wide information we will have an iMessage group chat with urgent matters being addressed by direct messaging or calling. 

4.  Response expectations: 

   - a.  Each team member should try to respond to a given message within 12 hours or sooner according to the urgency of the situation. 

   - b.  If a team member knows that they will be unable to respond in a given time they should inform the group of their absence prior. 

   - c.  If a team member misses a message in the given 12 hour window they should get in contact with another group member as soon as possible to receive the missing information. 

   - d.  If a team member consistently misses messages to an unreasonable degree they will be given a verbal warning by the team. If this behavior occurs again the team will reflect this unilaterally in the peer review. 

5.  Respect & Professional Conduct 

   - a.  Respectful behavior means to treat others as you would treat yourself. To respond to messages in a timely manner but also have the grace to understand the troubles that may plague another's life. 

   - b.  Behaviors that belittle or harm other team members are the height of what we find to be unacceptable. Other behaviors include: disrespectful treatment of other members, not responding to messages repeatedly and poor attendance of the team meetings. 

   - c.  To ensure proper and equal delegation of the project we will dedicate a small portion of all our meetings to allocate work amongst ourselves. 

6.  Accountability 

   - a.  If a member is unable to meet the expectation agreed upon by the team, we will have a meeting with the member to discuss what is going on. Additionally, we will discuss how this member is affecting the team as a whole. 

7.  Decision-Making 

      - a.  All majors decisions such as project direction will be made through group consensus. In a situation where there is disagreement in the group having the group members each explain why they believe x approach or idea is the correct solution till we can find a solution that all parties agree to. In the case where that is not possible we will go for a majority vote. 

8.  Conflict Resolution 

      - a.  Communication is essential for understanding different perspectives. We should always keep an open mind, listen to everyone’s ideas, and try to find a middle ground that everyone can agree on. If discussions take too long and we can’t reach a consensus, the final decision will be made through a majority vote. 

9.  Anticipated Challenges 

      - a.  We believe that our most anticipated challenge will be the lack of communication. We are all busy individuals with busy lives, and are all used to working alone, so we expect communication to be poor due to this. We will do our best to communicate when something is going on in our lives and are unable to make a meeting or deadline. 

      - b.  Schedule conflicts are another potential challenge for the same reasons stated above.
